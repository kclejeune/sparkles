# Golden files whose expected outputs were written by hand from the formatting rules
# before the printers that produce them exist. `cargo test -p sparkles-fmt --test golden`
# skips them (and never blesses them); `-- --ignored` checks them and reports the ones
# that pass, which then leave this list. One `<language>/<name>` per line (all its
# variants), whitespace, and the reason.
sparql/s1 needs the prologue, query, pattern, triples and expression printers
sparql/s2 needs the query, pattern, path and aggregate printers
sparql/s3 needs the VALUES, BIND, MINUS, NOT EXISTS and operator-chain printers
sparql/s4 needs the CONSTRUCT, triples and blank-node printers
sparql/s5 needs the update printers
sparql/s6 needs the RDF 1.2 term, VALUES and pragma printers
sparql/path-forms needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
sparql/pattern-comments needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
sparql/pattern-elements needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
sparql/triples-brackets needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
sparql/triples-layout needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
sparql/triples-rdf12 needs the prologue and query printers around the patterns
