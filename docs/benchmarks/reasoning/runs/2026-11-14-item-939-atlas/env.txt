host: atlas, a benchmark worker with the benchmark host's hardware
cpu: 13th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-13500
cpus: 20

rustc: rustc 1.99.1 (58cf8f9c3 2026-10-13)
build: mbx build --release -p sparkles-reasoner --example lubm at a63916c0, built on a development laptop, sha256 c117f5ba04555b9634fe5c05afac772b5e3ca400fa34674bc78b7e132397149b
arms: one binary. SPARKLES_SAMPLED_PLANNING=0 keeps the greedy order of every rule body and =1 orders bodies from sampled bindings, which is the default.
data: the example's databases of OWL2Bench with 1 and 5 universities (the generator's base.nt) and of LUBM(10) and LUBM(100) (UBA 1.7, seed 0, as scripts/bench-lubm.sh generates them), loaded with the example's loadfile command on the laptop and copied to atlas, so both hosts read the same blocks. long-bodies.rules, in this directory, is the file of six user rules with bodies of four to six atoms that run.sh.txt names.
commands: infer derives the closure in memory and prints the time of the rules (ms), the peak heap, a digest of the derived facts that does not depend on their order, and one that does. materialize runs the whole materialization on a fresh copy of the database and prints its time.
runs: one thread pinned to CPU 2 with taskset, or 20 threads on CPUs 0-19, each in a systemd scope with MemoryMax=13G, under ~/lanes/.heavy.lock with nothing else of the lanes running. The two arms alternate their order from round to round.
parts: part 1 ran LUBM(100) and OWL2Bench(5) with owl-rl at one thread and the first pair of OWL2Bench(5) at 20 threads, and was then stopped so that another job could take the host. Part 2 ran that block in full and the rest behind that job. Both parts keep every row.
load: atlas showed a load average of about 3 from tasks in uninterruptible sleep, with the CPUs idle. Part 2 started as the other job ended, so its first rows show the decaying average of that job.
later commits: the lane's commits after a63916c0 make the planner keep the greedy order in more cases and bound its reads of the store. They were not measured on atlas.
plan counts: plan-counts.txt holds the planner's count of plans that took a sampled order in each iteration, from the measured binary on the laptop's copies of the same databases.
correctness: every pair of infer runs has equal digests of its derived facts, and every pair of materialize runs, which print no digest, has equal inferred counts. summary.txt lists both.
